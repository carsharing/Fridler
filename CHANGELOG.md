# Changelog

## [0.1.0] Hello Fridge

- Home screen widget in small, medium and large: a fridge door, a sheet of
  paper with a dog-eared corner, a marker doodle and a pizza slice magnet.
- Write a short note on the paper from the app.
- Six fridge door colors: classic white, brushed steel, retro mint, butter
  cream, cherry red, midnight.
- Clear and Tinted home screens: the widget is drawn as a full-color picture
  so the system doesn't wash it out. Choose between keeping the fridge door or
  just the paper floating on the glass.
- "Behind the fridge" diagnostics: shows whether the app and widget found
  their shared storage after AltStore re-signed them, with a report to copy.
